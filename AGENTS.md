# AGENTS.md - Product-Specific Build Rules

Product: PeakLogic Field Evidence Loop

## Mission
Build the smallest competition MVP that turns a work order plus lightweight field media into a traceable evidence state and agentic next action.

## Non-negotiables
- OpenCV 5 must be substantive and verifiably present in the competition/CI runtime.
- Do not build a generic construction QA platform.
- Do not claim workmanship/safety certification.
- Do not invent manufacturer/legal/engineering requirements.
- Approved Proof-Point Templates own critical evidence requirements.
- Missing proof after concealment becomes an exception; never fabricate evidence.
- Photo and video use one downstream Evidence Engine.
- Video is optional; still-photo workflow must remain functional.
- Human approval stays explicit.

## Scope discipline
Follow `BUILD_PACKET.md` and `docs/BUILD_GATES.md`. Do not add UI/AWS/model work before the prior gate has evidence.
